This compact bank contains 26 recorded pitches in two velocity layers
(v8 and v15). The MP3 files were trimmed to seven seconds without re-encoding
and filenames were normalized. Other notes are pitch-shifted at playback;
the player applies gain envelopes and gentle compression.
